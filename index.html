About

I work on causal inference, experimentation, and behavioral economics, with a focus on consumer decision-making. Before Cornell I earned an M.A. at the University of Pittsburgh, where I coordinated the Pittsburgh Experimental Economics Laboratory and built and deployed experiment platforms used across multiple research groups.

Methods I use regularly: staggered difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, synthetic control, A/B and switchback experiment design, causal forests and meta-learners. Mostly in Python and SQL.
